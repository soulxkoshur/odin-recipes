Chai Recipe

This image displays the final look of Chai, a very popular indian beverage, if you follow it closely.

Ingredients Needed:

  • Water: 2 cups
  • Milk: 1 cup
  • Tea leaves: 2 teaspoons
  • Sugar: to taste
  • Ginger: 1 inch piece, crushed
  • Green cardamom: 3-4, crushed
  • Cinnamon stick: 1/2 inch piece
  • Cloves: 2
  • Fennel seeds: 1/2 teaspoon (optional)

Steps to follow:

  1. Take water in a saucepan and add crushed ginger, green cardamom, cinnamon stick, cloves and fennel seeds. Boil on a high flame for 2-3 minutes so the spices release their flavour.
  2. Add tea leaves and sugar to the boiling water. Let it boil for another 1-2 minutes.
  3. Pour in the milk and stir well. Bring the chai to a boil, then lower the flame and simmer for 3-4 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  4. Once the chai is well coloured and aromatic, switch off the flame. Strain it through a tea strainer into cups.
  5. Serve hot and enjoy your masala chai with biscuits or snacks.
  6. Enjoy!!
